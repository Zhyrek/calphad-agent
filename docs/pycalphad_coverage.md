# pycalphad 0.11.2 plotting / data-output coverage

This maps each item marked plotting / data-output / both in `docs/pycalphad_inventory.json` (117 of 152 items) to the chat-agent tool that provides it. **All of these tools work with `python_tool = "off"`** and need no internet. `pycalphad_help` gives the model offline docs, and `list_calphad_capabilities` gives it a task → tool map.

## Where each area is covered

### Equilibrium and properties

**`pycalphad.equilibrium` and `Workspace` (`get`, `get_dict`, `eq`, composition sets).** These are covered by:

* `calculate_equilibrium` for a small table of results;
* `equilibrium_property_plot` for 1-D or 2-D grids, CSV/netCDF output, line plots and maps;
* `export_equilibrium_data` for the raw dataset as `.nc` plus a tidy CSV.

**Property framework.** All of the following go through `equilibrium_property_plot`:

* `ModelComputedProperty`: GM, HM, SM, CPM, the `_MIX` properties, DOO, TC, BMAG, VM, and per-phase `PROP(PH)`;
* `JanssonDerivative` (e.g. `HM.T`, `X(PH,EL).T`);
* units, e.g. `HM[kJ/mol]` or `HM.T[J/g/K]`;
* `IsolatedPhase`, via `ISOLATED(PH):PROP`;
* `DormantPhase`, `DrivingForce` and the nucleus composition, via `DF(PH)` and `DORMANT(PH):PROP`;
* `T0`, via `T0(PH1,PH2)`;
* `ReferenceState`, via `ACR(EL,REFPH)` and `MUR(EL,REFPH)`.

**Variables.** `X`, `W`, `MU`, `NP` and `Y` are available both as conditions and as outputs in `equilibrium_property_plot`. `get_mass_fractions` and `get_mole_fractions` are behind `convert_composition`. W conditions are converted to X automatically when they are fixed values.

### Single phases, without equilibrium

**`pycalphad.calculate` and the Model energy/property attributes.** `phase_property_plot` covers:

* G/H/S/Cp and the `_MIX` properties of each phase against X at a fixed T;
* the lower-envelope and all-points views;
* common tangents;
* a property against T at a fixed composition;
* pure-element reference states, a replacement for the deprecated `shift_reference_state`.

**Symbolic model output.** `export_phase_model` covers:

* `Model.ast`, `models`, `excess_mixing_energy`, `endmember_reference_model` and `build_phase`;
* each contribution written as text or LaTeX, plus the parameter CSV and a point evaluation.

### Phase diagrams (mapping)

**`BinaryStrategy`, `TernaryStrategy`, `IsoplethStrategy`, `get_tieline_data`, `get_invariant_data`, `get_zpf_data`, `ZPFLine`, `Node` and `Point`.** These are covered by `map_phase_diagram`, which produces:

* the PNG, drawn with consistent phase colours;
* the boundary, tie-line and invariant CSVs, plus a JSON file;
* invariant reactions classified as eutectic, peritectic and so on;
* optional wt% and T–MU axes.

**Batch versions.** `plot_all_binaries` and `plot_all_ternaries` produce PowerPoint decks.

**`StepStrategy`, `get_data` and `plot_step`.** `step_map` gives any property against T, P or X, the transition temperatures and a CSV.

**`plot_binary`, `plot_ternary`, `plot_isopleth`, `plot_tielines`, `plot_invariants`, `phase_legend`, `TriangularAxes`, and `binplot`/`ternplot` with `return_strategy=True`.** These are reproduced from the extracted data by `map_phase_diagram`, which also exposes their options (tie-line density, `label_nodes`, axis limits). `plot_all_*` and `plot_diagram_with_data` call `binplot` directly.

**`eqplot`.** Available as `export_equilibrium_data` with `eqplot=true`.

### Other output

**Database output.** `Database.to_file`, `to_string`, `write_tdb`, `TCPrinter` and `reflow_text` are covered by `write_database`, which normalises a database (with `groupby` and `if_incompatible`) or writes an element/phase subset and reload-checks it. `Database.search` is covered by the parameter CSV from `export_phase_model` and by `tdb_info`.

**xarray results.** `to_netcdf`, `to_dataframe`, `sel` and `LightDataset.get_dataset` are covered by `export_equilibrium_data` and the CSVs from every tool. Any CSV can be re-plotted with `plot_csv`.

**Related packages.** `scheil.simulate_scheil_solidification`, `simulate_equilibrium_solidification` and `SolidificationResult` are covered by `scheil_solidification`. `espei.plot.dataplot` is covered by `plot_diagram_with_data`. `espei.plot.plot_endmember` and `plot_interaction` are covered by `plot_parameter_vs_data`.

## Deliberately not exposed as tools

* **The legacy `pycalphad.plot.binary.*` family** (`map_binary`, `plot_boundaries`, `ZPFBoundarySets`, `get_compsets`) and the legacy `pycalphad.plot.ternary.ternplot`. `pycalphad.mapping` supersedes them, and the tools above cover everything they produced.
* **Internal helpers:** `generate_dof`, `endmember_matrix`, `point_from_equilibrium` and `degrees_of_freedom`. They are used inside the tools, and have no standalone plotting or data-output purpose.
* **`get_label`.** It is a plotting internal, and the axis labels are generated by the tools.
* **`ModelMQMQA`** (quasichemical models from ChemSage `.dat` files). The equilibrium tools will load a `.dat` file through pycalphad's `Database`, but the TDB-text tools (`tdb_info`, `compare_tdbs`, `merge_tdbs`) need TDB files.

## Limitations of pycalphad 0.11.2 found while building these tools

* **Mapping conditions.** Isopleths with two composition axes, and mapping at a fixed chemical potential, are unsupported or untested upstream. T–MU diagrams are available only as a plot axis.
* **`NP(PH)=0` with T free.** A liquidus or solidus search of this kind can't be run as a Workspace condition. Use `step_map` or `scheil_solidification` to get those temperatures.
* **Ordered/disordered phases.** For ordered phases, `_MIX` properties come out as NaN. Ordered phases are also always reported under their ordered name, so the tools rename a phase to its disordered partner when DOO = 0.
* **netCDF output** uses the scipy (netCDF3) engine, because netCDF4/h5netcdf aren't installed.
